(QUESTIONS)

Parents usually ask.

01Which syllabus do you follow?+

The Mauritius national curriculum for Grades 7–9 (NCE), Cambridge SC for Grades 10–11 (Maths 4024, Add Maths 4037) and Cambridge HSC for Grades 12–13 (A Level Maths 9709). Groups are split by grade, so nobody sits through topics that aren't on their paper.

02Can my child join halfway through the year?+

Yes. The diagnostic paper on day one shows which earlier topics are missing, and those get covered in catch-up sessions alongside the group.

03What happens if a lesson is missed?+

Every lesson is recorded, and the notes and practice sheet are shared the same evening. A short catch-up can be booked if it's an important topic.

04How do I know it's working?+

Parents get a progress note every month with the topics covered, past paper marks, and what's next. You'll see the marks move before results day.

05Is the trial lesson really free?+

Yes. One full class plus the diagnostic paper, no payment details and no commitment. You decide afterwards.
